Our Story

A simple dream, still served fresh.

It all started with a simple dream in the late 1990s: to share the joy and authentic taste of paan with heartfelt service. Today Thakur Pan is a late-night corner at Akshar Chowk, where paan, soda, waffles and chai bring Akota together.

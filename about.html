About Us

Our Story

A neighbourhood paan corner that grew into one of Akota's favourite late-night stops.

The Origin

It started with one small dream

It all started with a simple dream in the late 1990s: to share the joy and authentic taste of paan with a heartfelt service.

From that small counter, Thakur Pan grew into the shop you see today at Earth Complex, beside the Akshar Chowk bridge. The menu has grown too, from paan and pan mawa soda to mojitos, shakes, waffles, chai and ice cream.

Late 1990s

The first counter opens with a simple dream.

Today

A lit-up corner at Akshar Chowk, open daily — till 11:30 PM Mon–Sat, 2:30 PM on Sundays.

Where every night ends with something sweet.
